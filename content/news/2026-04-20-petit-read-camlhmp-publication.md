[Robert Petit](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rpetit3/) and [Tim Read](/group/tim-read/) have published ["camlhmp: a simple framework for building reproducible microbial genome-based typing tools"](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/mra.00201-26) in *Microbiology Resource Announcements*. camlhmp (Classification through yAML Heuristic Mapping Protocol) provides a straightforward way to define and maintain organism typing schemas using YAML, making it easier to build consistent and reproducible typing tools for microbial genomics.
