[Tim Read](/group/tim-read/) is a co-author on a new publication: ["Phylogenetic diversity, functional pathways, and network interactions of ocular chlamydia-like organisms (CLOs) in trachoma-endemic Ethiopia"](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/mbio.00534-26) in *mBio*. The study sequenced ocular microbiomes from over 1,000 participants in Ethiopia, identifying six previously unknown chlamydia-like organism variants and finding that their presence was associated with lower rates of *Chlamydia trachomatis* infection and severe eye disease.
