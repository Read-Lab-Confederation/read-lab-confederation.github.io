[Michelle Su](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michelle-su-45b51a94/), [Kim Hoang](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kim-hoang-2627609a/), McKenna Penley, Michelle Davis, Jennifer Gresham, Levi Morran, and [Tim Read](/group/tim-read/) have published ["Host and antibiotic jointly select for greater virulence in *Staphylococcus aureus*"](https://elifesciences.org/reviewed-preprints/107936) in *eLife*. The study shows that *S. aureus* evolving under combined host and sub-inhibitory antibiotic pressure rapidly becomes more virulent, with mutations arising in key regulatory genes including *codY* and *agr*.
