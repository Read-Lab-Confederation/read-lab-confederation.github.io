Former lab member [Robert Petit](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rpetit3/) has released [Bactopia v4.0.0](https://bactopia.io/blog), a major update to his flexible bacterial genome analysis pipeline. The release features a complete rewrite for Nextflow 26.04, a new `gigatyper` tool for running all MLST schemes against an assembly, and updates to dozens of included tools.
